# Alstom: TF1-PP voltage transformer failures

Back to [[00 Index]] · Log: [[Projects/Alstom – TF1-PP Transformer/Decisions|Decisions]] · Sources: [[Sources/Summary – Alstom case deck]], [[Sources/Summary – Sponsor talks transcript#Alstom]]

## Goal
Find the most probable (ideally validated) root cause(s) of repeated in-service failures of the TF1-PP voltage measuring transformer on the roof of KZ8A electric locomotives. Then propose solutions, an implementation roadmap, and an economic analysis.

Analysis areas set by Alstom:
- electrical stress and transient overvoltage
- environment and climate
- installation and maintenance practices
- design and manufacturing
- operating conditions and mileage

## Audience
Azamat Utekeyev, Reliability Growth Manager (NU electrical engineering, 2016), plus Alstom talent acquisition. **Case leaders get a paid summer/winter internship.**

## Constraints
- Locomotive: KZ8A, 8.8 MW, 25 kV AC at 50 Hz, operating from −50 °C to +40 °C, about 200 in service in Kazakhstan.
- No failure data in the deck; details come only in Q&A.
- Needs high-voltage / reliability engineering knowledge to be convincing.

## Status
**Deprioritised (#3).** Reconsider only if the team is mostly EE or the internship is the main goal. Not started.
