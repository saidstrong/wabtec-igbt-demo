# Summary: Alstom case deck

**Original:** [[Originals/IEEE_case_championship_deck.pptx]] · **Project:** [[Projects/Alstom – TF1-PP Transformer/Brief|Alstom brief]]

**Company (slides 1–9):** recruiting pitch titled "Become a Rail Architect." Alstom, founded in 1928, covers three areas:
- rolling stock and components
- digital and integrated systems (signalling, turnkey projects, cybersecurity)
- services (maintenance, overhauls)

It operates in 63 countries with 254 sites and 10k+ patents. In Kazakhstan it runs the EKZ locomotive plant, 4 depots, a repair centre and a bogie centre. It has major KTZ contracts (speaker notes: 421 locomotives, 302 freight and 119 passenger) and exports to Azerbaijan and France. Focus areas: hydrogen and low-carbon trains, digital signalling, predictive maintenance and AI.

**Case input (slides 10–13), presented by Azamat Utekeyev, Reliability Growth Manager:**

KZ8A locomotive specs:
- 2 × BoBo, 8 axles, 2 × 100 t, 35 m long
- 25 kV AC at 50 Hz
- 8,800 kW traction / 7,600 kW electric braking
- up to 120 km/h, operating from −50 to +40 °C

Roof layout: the TF1-PP voltage measuring transformer sits alongside:
- the main circuit breaker and earthing switch
- surge arresters and filters
- the 25 kV bushing and insulators
- the current transformer

**Problem:** repeated failures of the TF1-PP, which is part of the 25 kV measurement and protection system.

**Objectives:**
- find the most probable root cause(s)
- propose solutions with an implementation roadmap
- provide an economic analysis

**Analysis areas:**
- electrical stress and transient overvoltage
- environment and climate
- installation and maintenance
- design and manufacturing
- operating conditions and mileage

**Prize:** case leaders are invited to a paid summer/winter internship.

> [!note]
> The deck has no failure statistics; more detail was promised in the Q&A.
